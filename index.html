User Agreement and Terms of Use

Please read carefully before using the SWS system.

1. Purpose

This system allows residents of Buina, Fittoa to submit water-related complaints and receive outage notifications from Sharia Water Services (SWS).

2. User responsibilities

  • Provide accurate and truthful information when registering and submitting complaints.
  • Do not submit false, harmful, or abusive content.
  • Keep your login credentials secure.
  • Use the system only for legitimate water service concerns.

3. Complaint handling

SWS will review and respond to complaints within reasonable timeframes. Status updates will be reflected in your dashboard.

4. Data privacy

Your personal information will be used only for processing complaints and sending outage notifications. We do not share your data with third parties without your consent.

5. Limitation of liability

SWS is not liable for delays or issues caused by circumstances beyond its control.

6. Acceptance of terms

By registering or using this system, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to these terms.
